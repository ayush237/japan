日本旅行

JAPAN · FIRST LIGHT IN AUTUMN

Delhi → Tokyo → Fuji → Izu → Nagoya → Hida → Kyoto → Osaka → Delhi

SAT 14 NOV — SUN 29 NOV 2026

Why this window

November wins. Peak Kyoto/Arashiyama foliage (~Nov 20–30), crystal-clear Mt Fuji visibility (Nov is statistically one of the best months), capybara onsen at its coziest, and winter illuminations switched on. 7–15°C is layer weather, not misery weather — every outdoor wishlist item survives. Flights priced ≈ same as October. 16 days door-to-door, flights on weekends, exactly 10 leave days used (Nov 16–20, 23–27).

DIY beats the operator: this plan totals ~₹4.4–4.9L for two vs the ₹5.8L quote — with a ryokan splurge, glamping and Ghibli Park included. Everything bookable with free cancellation is flagged.

Quiet moments ✦

Three low-key, standout spots if a certain question wants asking:
  • Mt Omuro crater rim, late afternoon (Day 7) — 360° sea-and-Fuji views, sparse crowds, golden light.
  • The ryokan evening, Izu (Day 7) — private open-air onsen, kaiseki, yukata, nobody else in the world.
  • Ine Bay waterfront (Day 13) — silent boat-houses on still water; the least touristed beautiful place on this route.

Booking deadlines

  • Ghibli Park: dated tickets, historically released ~2 months ahead on the 10th, sell out fast for holidays. verify
  • DisneySea: app sales open ~2 months ahead; buy date the moment it opens. verify
  • TeamLab Planets: ~2–3 months ahead; weekday afternoon slots last longest. verify
  • Shibuya Sky: ~4 weeks ahead; sunset slots vanish in minutes. verify
  • Saphir Odoriko: JR East reservations open 1 month before, 10:00 JST (Eki-net). verify
  • Aoniyoshi: Kintetsu bookings open 1 month ahead. verify
  • Sagano Train + Hozugawa boat: train seats 1 month ahead (JR West/stations); boat usually walk-up but pre-book peak foliage weekdays. verify
  • Sumo asageiko tour: 1–2 months ahead (limited stables/slots).
  • Ryokan veg kaiseki: request at booking; confirm again 7 days out.

Action plan · June → November

  • June: Confirm leave (Nov 16–20, 23–27). Book open-jaw flights (changeable fare). Buy insurance with cancellation cover same week. Apply for IDP (RTO/Parivahan — takes weeks; needed for go-karts, can't be issued in Japan).
  • July: Book ALL hotels free-cancel — ryokan and glamping first, Kyoto next (peak foliage rooms go earliest).
  • August: Book sumo practice tour. Sketch ticket-release calendar with exact dates.
  • September: Ghibli Park tickets on release day (~Sep 10 for late-Nov). Gather visa docs.
  • Early–mid Oct: Apply Japan visa via VFS (5–10 working days; both salaried = simple). DisneySea + TeamLab tickets on release.
  • Late Oct: Saphir Odoriko + Aoniyoshi + Sagano train at the 1-month mark. Shibuya Sky sunset slot. Kimono rental reservation.
  • Early Nov: Reconfirm ryokan veg kaiseki, Takayama/Shirakawa-gō bus seats, eSIM, forex card load, pack.

Visa checklist — Indian passport, salaried

  • Passport (6+ months validity, 2 blank pages) + old passports
  • Visa application form + 1 photo (45×45mm, white bg) each
  • Cover letter (joint, mentioning trip dates + self-funded)
  • Employment: NOC/leave-sanction letter on letterhead (JP Morgan / Oracle), 3 months salary slips
  • Bank statements (6 months, healthy balance) + ITR/Form 16 (2 yrs)
  • Day-wise itinerary (print this page!), hotel bookings, return flight booking
  • Apply via VFS Global Japan; typically 5–10 working days verify current fee/process

Packing — late November

  • Layers: thermals (1–2 sets), fleece/sweater, packable down jacket, light rain shell
  • Very comfortable walking shoes (20k+ steps/day) + warm socks; slip-on friendly for temples/ryokan
  • Beanie, light gloves, scarf (Shirakawa-gō + dawn Fushimi will bite)
  • Daypack, power bank, Type-A plug (same as some Indian 2-pin, but carry adapter), eSIM installed before flying
  • Forex card (Wise/Niyo) + ~¥20–30k cash for small shrines/buses
  • One nicer outfit (ryokan dinner ✦), swim-modesty not needed — onsens are bare, tattoo rules vary
  • IDP + Indian licence originals (go-karts), passport copies, insurance printout
  • Foldable duffel for souvenirs; medicines with prescription (check Japan's banned-meds list)
